# Recent-rain decision correction

A weather-only model must not let uncertainty about initial root-zone moisture outweigh strong evidence that a normal soil bed just received a substantial soaking.

For established in-ground and raised vegetable beds, 0.75 inch or more of recent observed rain is treated as a strong no-water signal when it is recent enough; 1.0 inch remains a strong no-water signal across the 72-hour observation window. A current gardener soil-feel observation still overrides this rule, and containers are excluded because they can dry much faster.

Recent-rain acquisition now checks multiple nearby NWS observation stations instead of depending on one station. The advanced panel also offers an optional rain-gauge override for localized storms that nearby stations miss.

Visual result states are intentionally distinct: green for WAIT, amber for CHECK SOIL FIRST, blue for HOLD FOR RAIN, and red for WATER TODAY. Color supplements, rather than replaces, the text decision.
