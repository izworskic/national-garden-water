# Research sources

Primary source contract:
- USDA NRCS Irrigation Water Management Plan — Small Farms and Gardens: root-zone deficit, soil texture/AWHC and rooting depth.
- USDA NRCS Soil Data Access: point map-unit/component/horizon properties.
- NWS NDFD FRET: daily and weekly reference crop evapotranspiration (`evp24`, `evp168`).
- NWS API: quantitative precipitation forecast and station observations.
- FAO-56: reference ET to crop ET using crop coefficients.

The production model must show uncertainty and must not claim actual soil moisture without a gardener observation or sensor.
