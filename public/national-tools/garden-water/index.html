What goes into the call

Water in

Observed and incoming rain

Recent NOAA and NWS observations are reconciled before the decision. Forecast probability is never turned into an invented rainfall amount.

Water out

Recent drying and today's demand

Observed temperatures drive the recent water-loss ledger. NWS reference evapotranspiration is preferred for today's atmospheric demand.

Water storage

Soil and root-zone context

USDA mapped soil and crop rooting depth estimate storage. The tool carries uncertainty as a range rather than pretending it measured your soil moisture.

This is a weather-and-soil decision model, not a soil-moisture sensor. If you watered recently or have a rain-gauge reading, add it under “Adjust this result for my garden.”
