Garden Water Today

Enter a city or ZIP. The tool checks recent National Weather Service rain, the next rain, atmospheric water demand, and mapped soil — then tells you whether a typical established vegetable garden needs water today.

What this decision uses

Recent rainObserved NWS station precipitation where available, plus the local NWS grid.
Next rainNWS hourly precipitation probability and quantitative precipitation forecast.
Drying demandReference evapotranspiration estimated from NWS temperature, humidity, wind, solar proxy and elevation.
Soil contextUSDA Web Soil Survey map units near your garden, including drainage and available-water context where the source provides it.

This is a field decision aid, not a soil-moisture sensor. Container gardens, seedlings, newly transplanted crops, sandy fill, raised beds and localized thunderstorms can differ substantially from the mapped estimate. Check the top few inches of soil before making a large irrigation change.
