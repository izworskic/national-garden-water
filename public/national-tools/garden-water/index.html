What the tool checks automatically

Water in

Recent and incoming rain

Observed National Weather Service precipitation is paired with quantitative forecast rainfall. Rain probability is never converted into an invented amount.

Water out

Atmospheric demand

NWS reference evapotranspiration is preferred. If it is unavailable, the tool uses a labeled weather-based fallback and lowers confidence.

Water storage

Mapped soil context

USDA NRCS soil properties help estimate how much water the root zone can hold. Containers use a separate potting-media assumption instead of yard soil.

This is a weather-and-soil model, not a soil-moisture sensor. The automatic answer assumes no hose or sprinkler watering in the past week unless you tell it otherwise.
